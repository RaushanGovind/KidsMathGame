## How Colors Are Applied

The colors cycle through based on position:
- Bubbles 1, 11, 21, 31, etc. → Color 1 (Red)
- Bubbles 2, 12, 22, 32, etc. → Color 2 (Orange)
- Bubbles 3, 13, 23, 33, etc. → Color 3 (Yellow)
- And so on...

This creates a beautiful rainbow pattern across your bubble matrix!

## Special Effects

✨ **Pop Animation**: When you click a bubble, it bounces with a scale animation
🌟 **Glow Effect**: Selected bubbles have a soft glow around them
🎨 **Gradients**: Each bubble has a smooth color gradient from light to dark
💫 **Hover Effect**: Bubbles grow slightly bigger when you hover over them
🔄 **Rotate on Hover**: Selected bubbles rotate 5° when hovered
