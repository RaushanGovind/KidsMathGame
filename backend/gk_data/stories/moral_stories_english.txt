1. The Honest Boy
Ravi found a wallet on the road. It had money and an ID card.
He took it to his teacher. The teacher called the owner.
The man was very happy and thanked Ravi.
Ravi felt proud because he was honest.
Moral: Honesty is the best policy.


2. The Thirsty Crow
A crow was very thirsty. He found a pot with little water.
He could not reach the water. He put small stones into the pot.
The water came up. The crow drank and flew away happily.
Moral: Think smart and never give up.


3. The Lazy Cat
Mimi the cat did not like to work. She slept all day.
One day, she had no food. She felt very hungry.
She learned to catch food and never stayed lazy again.
Moral: Laziness brings trouble.


4. The Helpful Friend
Amit fell while playing. He hurt his knee.
His friend Rohan helped him stand and took him home.
Amit said, "Thank you for helping me."
Moral: A friend in need is a friend indeed.


5. The Greedy Dog
A dog had a bone. He saw his reflection in the water.
He thought another dog had a bigger bone.
He barked and dropped his own bone into the water.
Moral: Don't be greedy.


6. The Brave Little Girl
Sara was afraid of the dark. One night, the lights went out.
She took a deep breath and walked slowly to get a candle.
She felt brave and happy.
Moral: Courage grows when we try.


7. The Sharing Tree
A big tree gave shade, fruits, and wood to a boy.
The boy grew up and forgot the tree.
One day, he came back tired. The tree gave him a place to rest.
Moral: Always be thankful.


8. The Clever Rabbit
A lion scared all the animals. A rabbit made a plan.
He showed the lion his reflection in a well.
The lion jumped in and could not come out.
All animals were safe.
Moral: Intelligence is stronger than strength.


9. The Lost Puppy
A puppy lost his way home. He felt scared.
A kind girl saw him and read his collar name.
She called the number and the puppy went home safely.
Moral: Kindness saves lives.


10. The Little Seed
A small seed was under the soil. It wanted to see the sun.
It pushed up slowly and became a green plant.
After some time, it became a big tree.
Moral: Patience helps us grow.

11. The Honest Woodcutter
A woodcutter dropped his axe into a river. He felt very sad.
A fairy appeared and showed him a golden axe. He said, "This is not mine."
She showed a silver axe. He again said no.
Finally, she showed his old iron axe. He smiled and said, "Yes, this is mine."
The fairy was happy and gave him all three axes.
Moral: Always tell the truth.


12. The Foolish Donkey
A donkey wore a lion's skin and scared many animals.
One day, he started to bray loudly.
The animals knew he was a donkey and chased him away.
Moral: Pretending to be someone else will not last.


13. The Two Friends and the Bear
Two friends were walking in a forest. A bear came near them.
One friend climbed a tree. The other lay still on the ground.
The bear smelled him and went away, thinking he was dead.
Later, the friend asked, "What did the bear say?"
He replied, "Never trust a friend who leaves you in danger."
Moral: A true friend stays in trouble.


14. The Magic Words
A boy never said "please" or "thank you."
One day, no one helped him because he was rude.
He learned to speak politely. Soon, everyone liked him.
Moral: Politeness costs nothing.


15. The Wise Owl
Animals argued about who was the best.
The owl listened quietly and said, "Everyone has a special talent."
The animals understood and stopped fighting.
Moral: Everyone is unique.


16. The Boy Who Cried Wolf
A boy shouted, "Wolf! Wolf!" for fun.
Villagers ran to help but found no wolf.
One day, a real wolf came. The boy cried for help.
No one came, and the sheep ran away.
Moral: Never tell lies.


17. The Proud Rose
A rose laughed at a cactus for being ugly.
One hot summer, there was no water.
Birds drank water from the cactus.
The rose felt sorry and learned not to judge others.
Moral: Never make fun of others.


18. The Little Red Hen
A hen found some wheat and asked others to help plant it.
"No," said the cat, dog, and duck.
The hen worked alone and made bread.
When others wanted to eat, she said, "No, you did not help."
Moral: Those who work hard earn rewards.


19. The Golden Egg
A farmer had a hen that laid one golden egg every day.
He became greedy and thought there were many eggs inside.
He cut the hen open but found nothing.
He lost even the one egg a day.
Moral: Greed brings loss.


20. The Rainy Day
It rained all day, and Riya felt bored.
She read a book, drew pictures, and helped her mother.
By evening, she felt happy and proud.
Moral: Make good use of your time.


21. The Lost Pencil
Rohan lost his pencil in class. He felt sad.
His friend Meena gave him her extra pencil.
Rohan said, "Thank you!"
Moral: Sharing makes friends happy.


22. The Clean Room
Tina's room was very messy. She could not find her toy.
Her mother said, "Clean your room."
Tina cleaned and found her toy under the bed.
Moral: Keep your things neat.


23. The Hungry Bird
A little bird was hungry. She looked for food.
She found some seeds near a house.
She ate and sang happily.
Moral: Hard work brings food.


24. The Broken Toy
Arjun's toy car broke. He wanted to throw it away.
His father fixed it with glue.
Arjun was happy to play again.
Moral: Fix things instead of wasting them.


25. The Good Habit
Neha brushed her teeth every morning and night.
Her teeth stayed clean and white.
The doctor said, "Very good!"
Moral: Good habits keep us healthy.


26. The Helpful Son
Rahul saw his mother carrying heavy bags.
He ran and helped her.
His mother smiled and hugged him.
Moral: Help your parents.


27. The Little Cloud
A small cloud felt useless in the sky.
Soon, it rained and helped plants grow.
The cloud felt proud.
Moral: Everyone has a purpose.


28. The New Student
A new boy came to school. He felt shy.
A girl said, "Come, sit with me."
They became good friends.
Moral: Be kind to new people.


29. The Early Riser
Vikram woke up early every day.
He had time to play and study.
He never felt rushed.
Moral: Waking up early is good.


30. The Thank You Note
Maya got a gift from her aunt.
She wrote a thank you note.
Her aunt felt very happy.
Moral: Always say thank you.


31. The Missing Shoe
Kunal could not find one shoe. He looked under the bed.
He looked behind the door. It was inside his school bag!
He laughed and wore his shoes.
Moral: Keep your things in the right place.


32. The Small Candle
The light went out at night. It was very dark.
Mother lit a small candle. The room became bright.
Everyone felt safe.
Moral: Even a small light removes darkness.


33. The Happy Plant
Ritu watered her plant every day.
The plant grew tall and green.
It had beautiful flowers.
Moral: Care brings growth.


34. The Silent Library
Children were talking loudly in the library.
The teacher said, "This is a place to read."
They spoke softly and enjoyed their books.
Moral: Follow rules in quiet places.


35. The Big Umbrella
It started raining after school.
A boy shared his big umbrella with two friends.
They reached home dry and happy.
Moral: Sharing helps everyone.


36. The Little Helper
Grandfather dropped his glasses.
Anya quickly picked them up and gave them to him.
Grandfather smiled and blessed her.
Moral: Respect and help elders.


37. The Tidy Desk
Aman's desk was full of papers and toys.
He cleaned it and arranged his books.
Now he could study easily.
Moral: A tidy place helps us work better.


38. The Friendly Dog
A dog came near the park every day.
Children gave him water and food.
The dog wagged his tail happily.
Moral: Be kind to animals.


39. The Careful Walker
Riya always looked right and left before crossing the road.
One day, she stopped just in time as a bike passed.
She crossed safely.
Moral: Be careful on the road.


40. The Smile
Kabir was sad at school.
His friend smiled and shared a joke.
Kabir laughed and felt better.
Moral: A smile can make someone's day.
