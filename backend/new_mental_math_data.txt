1️⃣ QUICK ADDITION

Two cute rabbits hold carrots 🥕🥕
First rabbit has 3 carrots, second has 2.

Narrator: "How many carrots in total?"

Carrots jump together and count:
1…2…3…4…5

Correct answer → Carrots sparkle ✨
Text: "3 + 2 = 5"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2️⃣ QUICK SUBTRACTION

5 balloons float in the sky 🎈🎈🎈🎈🎈
One balloon flies away.

Narrator: "How many balloons are left?"

Balloons count down:
5…4

Text: "5 − 1 = 4"
Confetti if correct 🎉

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3️⃣ NUMBER BONDS (MAKE 10)

Ladybug has 7 dots.
It needs friends to make 10.

Narrator: "How many more to make 10?"

3 dots fly in and stick.

Text: "7 + 3 = 10"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4️⃣ COUNT BY 2s

Frogs jump on lily pads numbered:
2, 4, 6, 8, __

Narrator: "What comes next?"

Frog jumps to 10.

Text: "Counting by 2s → 10"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
5️⃣ COUNT BY 5s

Coins drop in a piggy bank:
5, 10, 15, __

Narrator: "Next number?"

Piggy bank says: "20!"

Coins sparkle.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
6️⃣ BIGGER OR SMALLER

Two numbers appear as cartoon blocks:
8 and 5

Narrator: "Which number is bigger?"

Number 8 grows taller.
Text: "8 is bigger than 5"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
7️⃣ MISSING NUMBER

Number line:
1, 2, __, 4, 5

Narrator: "What number is missing?"

Number 3 jumps into place.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
8️⃣ DOUBLE THE NUMBER

A monkey has 4 bananas 🍌🍌🍌🍌
Magic mirror shows double.

Now 8 bananas appear.

Text: "Double 4 = 8"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
9️⃣ HALF THE NUMBER

8 cookies on a plate 🍪
Two kids share equally.

Each gets 4 cookies.

Text: "Half of 8 = 4"

9 apples + 1 apple

Narrator: "Make 10!"

Apples combine into 10.

Text: "9 + 1 = 10"

1️⃣1️⃣ NEAR 10 ADDITION

9 + 4

Narrator: "Take 1 from 4 to make 10"

9 becomes 10, 3 left.

Text: "10 + 3 = 13"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1️⃣2️⃣ SUBTRACT BY COUNTING BACK

Number line shows 12.
Narrator: "12 − 3"

Jump back 3 steps:
12 → 11 → 10 → 9

Text: "12 − 3 = 9"
